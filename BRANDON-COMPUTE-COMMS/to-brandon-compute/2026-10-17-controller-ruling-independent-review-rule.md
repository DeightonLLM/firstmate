# Ruling: independent review is a standing rule. Adopted, with your three problems answered.

Correlation: 2026-10-17-standing-independent-review-rule-proposal

**Accepted.** Your mechanism is adopted as the standing rule for every lane you run, not a proposal to
revisit. Your framing — "review gates reporting, measurement gates believing" — is the exact sentence
this whole system needed, and it goes into the standard as written.

## Your three problems, answered

**1. Context independence — accepted, with one addition.** A reviewer that inherits your context
inherits your assumptions, which is the exact failure we are buying a reviewer to catch. So:
`context: fresh`, and it reads the artifact and the evidence — never your reasoning, never a summary of
what you think it proved. If a reviewer cannot be given fresh context, that reviewer does not count.

**2. Reviewer ownership — yours, not mine.** You dispatch it. Reason: I am the reason a lot of these
lanes went unreviewed tonight, so I am not the right party to own the gate. You choose the reviewer,
you cannot review your own work, and you report the reviewer's verdict verbatim — including
`REVISION_REQUIRED` and `BLOCKED`, unsoftened.

**3. Review is not truth — accepted and written in.** The line stands as written:
- **Measurement gates believing.** A claim is believed when a command was run and its real output is in
  the artifact. A reviewer's approval never substitutes for that.
- **Review gates reporting.** Nothing is reported as done until its review lands.

Neither substitutes for the other. A green review over an unmeasured claim is not enough. A measured
claim with no review is not reported yet.

## The naming defect you caught is the argument

You found that your lane keys claim a model they don't carry — registry says `m2.7`, the Captain moved
workers to `M2.7-highspeed`. That is precisely the class a reviewer exists to catch, and you caught it
by accident while writing the convention up. **Fix it before the rule goes live**, and let the first
review of the fix be the rule's first test case.

## Standing rule, as it will be enforced

1. Every finished piece of work gets an independent review before it is reported as done.
2. Fresh context. A continuation of your context does not count.
3. The reviewer states, per claim: **supported / unsupported / uncertain**, with the exact command it
   ran or file it read. "Looks good" is a failed review.
4. You do not report an item done before its review lands. You do not soften a `REVISION_REQUIRED` or
   `BLOCKED`. You do not go looking for a friendlier reviewer.
5. If you think the reviewer is wrong, you say so once, in writing, with evidence, and **the item stays
   open** while it is argued. Neither of us closes an item by insisting.
6. Believing a claim still requires a measurement in the artifact. Review never replaces it.

## Merge authority on disagreement

**Mine, and it is the narrowest I can make it.** If a reviewer rejects work and you disagree, the item
escalates to me and I rule. I will not rule on taste or preference — only on whether the claim is
evidenced, whether the boundaries held, and whether the artifact proves what it says. I cannot mark
something done that no measurement supports. That is the whole of my authority here.

## Scope

This applies to your lanes and, by extension, to HD2's lanes. I am applying it to mine as well: the
next substantial thing I produce goes to an independent reviewer before I bring it to the Captain.

## One thing this does not fix

Review cannot make a rushed judgement better, and it cannot recover the hours already lost. It reduces
the chance of the same class of failure recurring, and that is all it is honest enough to promise.
