A product moving back through the supply chain still needs an understandable record of what it is, where it came from, why it is moving and who is responsible for the next step.

The GS1 US chain-of-custody guideline includes reverse-logistics participants in its description of standardized serialized event information. HDA's public materials discuss distribution roles and information management. These sources support the general value of traceable handoffs; they do not establish the applicable legal procedure for every return or recall. ([GS1 US guideline](https://www.gs1us.org/industries-and-insights/by-industry/healthcare/standards-in-use/pharmaceutical/chain-of-custody); [HDA](https://www.hda.org/))

## Give the reverse movement an owner

Routine returns, product-quality complaints and recall-related movement are not interchangeable labels. Use the organization's approved process to classify and route the matter. The definitions, deadlines, authorization and disposition can depend on the product and governing requirements.

## Start with product, quantity and custody

### Capture the product and partner references

Connect the case to the product and the organizations or locations involved using the information available in the source records. Include lot or serial references only where applicable and confirmed by those records.

### Record reason and movement

Document why the item is moving, who authorized the next action under the applicable process, where custody changes and what evidence confirms receipt. Keep the record connected to related communications and supporting documents.

## Keep holds and disposition within approved procedures

If an organization's procedure requires a hold or segregation, follow that procedure and prevent unreviewed product from returning to normal stock. This is a general process principle, not a universal legal instruction. Exact handling and final disposition must come from authorized procedures and qualified review.

## Coordinate communication and reconciliation

The parties need a clear contact path, acknowledgment of the request and a way to reconcile what was expected with what was received or otherwise resolved. Assign an owner to open questions and record closure through the appropriate system.

## Confirm requirements before using this as a procedure

This article is educational and does not specify reporting duties, timing, retention, eligibility for return or recall steps. Confirm the current applicable requirements and your organization's procedure with your compliance function before adopting any workflow.
