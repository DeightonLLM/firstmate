## Correlated-failure finding

Only one sibling finding was available, so **there is insufficient evidence to say multiple sibling lanes actually repeated one identical error**. The concrete shared-error candidate that must be checked in every arriving report is nevertheless clear: **conflating “not evidenced” or “curl produced no HTTP response” with “the service/host/DNS record does not exist.”** The status brief makes that leap; the sibling audit catches it. If other lanes repeat the leap, that is the correlated failure mode—not independent corroboration.

A second fleet-wide risk to check is **citation laundering**: traceability and the delivery map sometimes cite each other or the same manifest/artifact. Two documents repeating one status do not constitute two independent measurements. Likewise, a “BLOCKED” inventory item means the evidence/coordinates are missing, not that the real-world service is absent. No claim of repetition across missing sibling reports is made.

## Provenance fabrication risks

- **“External measurement 2026-10-18” / “DNS: curl exit”** is a source label without a checkable evidence artifact. Without command, timestamp/time zone, resolver, query type, raw response/exit output, and host/path tested, readers cannot independently verify what was measured. It can falsely signal that DNS nonexistence was established.
- **“No source” / “not present” / “not resolving”** in the status brief mixes absence of evidence with positive negative findings. Use `UNKNOWN` when sources were not found; describe only measured endpoint responses as observations.
- **MD5 equality** is a weakly documented provenance claim without the two captured bodies/commands; even if true, equality only compares the measured responses and does not prove deployment ownership or source state.
- **Source-index pointers to `SERVICE_CATALOG.md` and `HOSTS.md`** are claims the brief says it consulted; they are outside the four-source baseline I was tasked to read and were not independently checked in this review. Do not relay those citations as independently verified evidence.
