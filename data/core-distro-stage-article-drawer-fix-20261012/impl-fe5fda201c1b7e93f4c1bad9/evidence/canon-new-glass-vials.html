Specifications

KEY FEATURES & STANDARDS

Type I Borosilicate Glass

Superior chemical resistance and thermal stability. Meets USP Type I standards for pharmaceutical containers.

Clear & Amber Options

Clear glass for standard preparations. Amber glass provides UV protection for light-sensitive compounds.

RTU (Ready-to-Use)

Pre-washed, depyrogenated, and sterilized vials ready for immediate use in your cleanroom environment.

RTS (Ready-to-Sterilize)

Pre-washed vials ready for your in-house sterilization cycle. Cost-effective for high-volume operations.

ISO 8362-1 Compliant

Full compliance with international standards for glass containers for injectable preparations.
