Regulatory Standards

THE STANDARDS WE UPHOLD.

21 CFR Part 211

Current GMP for Finished Pharmaceuticals

All packaging and equipment suppliers comply with FDA's current Good Manufacturing Practice regulations.

USP <797>

Pharmaceutical Compounding — Sterile Preparations

Our packaging components meet USP <797> requirements for sterile compounding environments.

USP <800>

Hazardous Drugs — Handling in Healthcare Settings

Compliance support for pharmacies handling hazardous drug compounds.

ISO 8362-1

Injection Containers for Injectables

All glass vials meet ISO 8362-1 standards for injectable pharmaceutical containers.

All suppliers undergo rigorous on-site GMP audits before any product enters our supply chain. We verify manufacturing practices, quality systems, and regulatory compliance.

Complete lot traceability from manufacturer to your pharmacy. Every product can be traced back to its origin, manufacturing date, and quality release records.
