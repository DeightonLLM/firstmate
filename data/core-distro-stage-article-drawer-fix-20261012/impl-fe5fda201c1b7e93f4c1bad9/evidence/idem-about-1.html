About Core Distro

THE INFRASTRUCTURE

Core Distro was built to solve a real problem: compounding pharmacies spending too much time managing fragmented suppliers. We are the single, trusted partner for sterile packaging and fill-finish automation.

Our Mission

SIMPLIFYING THE SUPPLY
CHAIN FOR COMPOUNDERS.

Core Distro was founded with a clear mission: to provide compounding pharmacies with a reliable, compliant, and comprehensive supply chain solution. We understand the regulatory complexities, the quality demands, and the operational pressures that compounders face every day.

By combining global sourcing expertise with deep FDA regulatory knowledge, we built a portfolio that supports the entire compounding process — from raw ingredient to finished product. We act as an extension of your purchasing department, familiar with the realities of 503A and 503B operations.
