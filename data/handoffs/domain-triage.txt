when it was visibly broken.

WHAT TO DO
1. Establish what actually exists for YOUR domain, with evidence:

2. Browser-check any web surface you own. Screenshots, desktop and mobile. A curl 200 is

4. Give a ranked list of the next three things worth doing, most valuable first, with the

- Every claim needs a second signal. Status plus artifact, branch plus ancestry, pane plus

- If you cannot verify it, say UNVERIFIED. Do not round it up to done.
- Never invent a statistic, a customer, or a completion percentage.
- If something is unreachable, say exactly which host, which path, and the error verbatim.
