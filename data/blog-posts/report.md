# Core Distro blog package report

## Outcome

Wrote ten evidence-bounded Markdown drafts under `posts/`, with front matter and dates from the research base. Each post follows its planned angle and outline, cites only research-base sources opened during the earlier research, and avoids adding unsupported metrics or regulatory dates. The drafts use plain-language descriptions and do not claim Core Distro-specific product, service, facility or performance details that the research did not verify.

Other compliance-adjacent drafts avoid procedural requirements, product thresholds, retention periods, legal consequences and regulatory timelines.

- Universal cold-chain thresholds, excursion windows, product disposition rules, record retention periods and required onboarding documents.
- Unverified Core Distro product, certification, facility, service, system, contact or operational claims.
- Unverified internal routes; drafts retain `[VERIFY: ...]` markers rather than inventing links.
- Unsplash images that were bright, blue/cool-lit, generic, unrelated, potentially misleading or failed the brand image filter.
