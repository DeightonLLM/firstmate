# Core Distro blog: market research and 10-post plan

**Status:** Research and outlines only; no post copy written.

**Requested schedule:** ten post dates starting 1 January 2026, roughly weekly.

**Research method:** Opened the cited public pages in `chrome-devtools-axi`. The Core Distro production and staging sites were not opened or changed. This is a retrospective editorial plan, not evidence that any post was actually published on its proposed date.

## Executive verdict

**VERDICT: Solid enough for an editorial outline and positioning plan; not yet solid enough to write or publish compliance guidance.** The reached distributor and trade-association pages support useful themes around pharmaceutical distribution, specialty products, logistics, customer needs, chain-of-custody, and supply-chain data. However, this research session could not reach the FDA DSCSA page, an EU GDP page, the attempted WHO GDP record, or GS1 Global's healthcare page. The accessible sources do not replace current primary regulatory texts or an expert review. Before writing DSCSA, GDP, temperature-excursion, or other compliance claims, the writer must verify the exact jurisdiction, effective dates, and current primary-source requirements. Do not transfer today's page content or regulatory developments into a post dated earlier in 2026 without confirming that the information existed on that proposed date.

The market list below is a **relevance-based shortlist, not a verified traffic, revenue, or market-share ranking**. No ranking dataset was found or measured. “Top five” should not be represented as an objective popularity ranking.

### 4. When a Cold-Chain Excursion Happens: The Evidence and Decisions to Capture — 2026-01-22

- **H2: An alert is a signal to investigate—not a disposition** — State that product-specific labeling, validated procedures and qualified quality judgment control; no generic threshold.
- **H2: First response: protect product and preserve evidence**
  - **H3: Identify the affected shipment and lot** — Link shipment, product, location and time window.
  - **H3: Preserve logger and handling records** — Capture device status, timestamps, packaging and custody data.
- **H2: Assess with the right technical input** — Escalate to manufacturer/quality team and use product-specific stability information; don't invent safe time-temperature windows.
- **H2: Record the decision and disposition** — Quarantine/hold where procedure requires, documented decision owner, release/return/destruction paths as applicable.
- **H2: Close the loop** — Root-cause categories, corrective action and partner communication.
