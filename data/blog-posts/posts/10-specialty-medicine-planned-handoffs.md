A special handling need is useful only when the right people know what it is and how to act on it. Before a shipment, the pharmacy and distribution partner can confirm the product-specific instructions, receiving site, responsible contacts and exception path.

Cencora describes distribution and services for pharmacy, provider and health-system settings. McKesson describes distribution across care settings and handling capabilities, and Morris & Dickson describes specialty-pharmacy services. These company pages are useful context for the range of settings distributors describe serving; they do not establish requirements for every specialty medicine or prove any particular shipment capability. ([Cencora](https://www.cencora.com/); [McKesson](https://www.mckesson.com/business-solutions/pharmaceutical-medical-products-distribution/); [Morris & Dickson specialty pharmacies](https://www.morrisdickson.com/services/specialty-pharmacies/))

## Start with product-specific instructions

Use the product's applicable labeling and manufacturer instructions, together with the pharmacy's approved procedures. A general article cannot supply a temperature range, delivery window or stability decision for an individual medicine.

## Coordinate the order and delivery plan

### Confirm the receiving site

Check the delivery location, receiving hours and authorized contact with the people responsible for receipt. Confirm how the parties will handle an anticipated delay or an unsuccessful delivery.

### Name handling and escalation owners

Agree who receives and acts on a special handling alert, delivery exception or product question. Make the contact path available to the people who need it, including the appropriate after-hours route if the parties' process provides one.

### Plan for records and continuity

Confirm how the buyer can find order status, delivery evidence and the records needed for its internal process. Ask what happens if the named contact is unavailable or an exception occurs.

## Consider the site-of-care handoff

A product may move through a pharmacy, clinic, infusion center or another care setting. The relevant parties and handoffs depend on the arrangement. Identify who receives the shipment, who takes responsibility for the next step and how questions move between those teams.

## Pre-shipment discussion checklist

- Are the product and destination clearly identified?
- Have the responsible parties reviewed the applicable product instructions?
- Is the receiving contact confirmed?
- Is there an agreed contact for delay or delivery exceptions?
- Can the buyer access the order and receipt information it needs?
- Are unresolved questions assigned to a named owner?

This checklist is a general coordination aid, not regulatory advice or a substitute for product-specific procedures. Confirm applicable requirements with your own compliance function.
